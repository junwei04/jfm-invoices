Privacy Policy

JFM Invoices — J Facilities Management Pte. Ltd.

This app is an internal tool for J Facilities Management Pte. Ltd. staff only. It is not available to the general public.

Data storage

All data — including invoices, quotes, client details, and business information — is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage on your own device. No data is sent to or stored on any external server by this application.

Google Calendar integration

If you choose to use the Calendar Booking Import feature, the app connects to a private Google Apps Script proxy that you set up and control. The proxy reads your Google Calendar events and returns them to this app running on your device. No calendar data is stored anywhere other than your device's local memory during the session.

Third-party services

This app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or any third-party data services.
