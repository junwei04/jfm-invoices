JFM Invoices

Internal quoting & invoicing tool for J Facilities Management Pte. Ltd.

JFM Invoices is a private tool built for J Facilities Management Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202553962R), a facilities management and construction services company in Singapore. It lets our team create and send quotations and invoices to clients directly from a phone or computer.

What it does

  • Creates quotations and invoices for facilities management and construction services, with line items, discounts, and deposits.
  • Generates a PDF with a PayNow QR code and bank transfer details for client payment.
  • Includes dual sign-off blocks for both parties on all documents, as required for commercial and government records.
  • Optionally reads upcoming bookings from the user's own Google Calendar to pre-fill client details.

Who uses it

This is an internal business tool used only by J Facilities Management Pte. Ltd. staff. It is not a public product or service.
